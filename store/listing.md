## Short description (max 80)
Smart packing lists from your trip and the weather. Pack one category at a time.

## Full description (max 4000)
Packing shouldn't be stressful. PackGo builds your packing list from who's going, where you're headed, when, and what the weather will be like. Then it helps you pack calmly, one category at a time.

★ A LIST THAT KNOWS YOUR TRIP
Tell PackGo who's travelling (adults, kids, babies), where you're going and the kind of trip: beach, city, hiking, snow or work. It picks the right items and the right amounts for how long you're away.

★ REAL WEATHER, ANY CITY
Search any city in the world. PackGo checks the live forecast, and for trips further ahead it uses last year's weather on the same dates. Rain on Thursday? The umbrella is already on your list.

★ ONE CATEGORY AT A TIME
Focus mode shows just one category at a time, so a long list never feels overwhelming. Tick items off, use Select all, or skip and come back later.

★ MADE FOR REAL PACKING
• Swipe left to delete, swipe right to mark "often forgotten"
• Change quantities with + / −
• Undo for every delete
• Add your own items. They carry over to your next trip
• Hide packed items in the full list

★ ALL YOUR TRIPS
Plan several trips at once and see how far along each one is.

★ REMINDERS
Get a reminder a week, 3 days or the night before you leave, at the time you choose, showing how much is left to pack.

★ SHARE WITH YOUR TRAVEL PARTNER
Send your list as text to any chat, or save it as an image.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your trips stay on your phone.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.
