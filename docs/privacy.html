PackGo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

PackGo is a packing list app. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: your trips and lists stay on your phone. PackGo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in the app's storage on your phone:

  • Your trips: destination, dates, number and age range of travellers, gender, trip type and reminder settings
  • Your packing lists: items you tick off, quantities you change, items you add or remove, and items you mark as "often forgotten"

We (the developer) cannot see this information. It is deleted when you delete a trip or uninstall the app.

Information sent to the weather service

To find places and show the weather, PackGo uses Open-Meteo, a free weather service:

  • When you search for a destination, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search.
  • To load the weather, the destination's coordinates and your travel dates are sent to Open-Meteo.

Like any website, Open-Meteo receives your device's IP address when the app connects to it. PackGo does not use your phone's location (GPS) and does not send your name, contacts or any account details. See Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, PackGo schedules notifications on your phone (for example, the day before you leave). These are created on your device; no notification data is sent to us or to any server.

Sharing

When you tap "Share list" or "Save as image", PackGo opens your phone's share menu. Your list only goes where you choose to send it.

Permissions

  • Internet: to search places and load the weather.
  • Notifications (and exact alarms): to show the reminders you choose.
  • Vibration: for small haptic feedback when you tick items.

Children

PackGo is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
